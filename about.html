01 / THE THROUGH-LINE

Practical problems.
Theoretical questions.

Before graduate school, I worked as a structural design engineer on hydropower infrastructure in Nepal, where designing structures meant working with difficult ground conditions, seismic demands, constructability, and multiple engineering disciplines.

That experience made computational models feel less abstract: they are useful only insofar as they tell us something meaningful about the structure or decision in front of us.

Today, my research spans structural dynamics, system identification, numerical modeling, machine learning, and broader questions of seismic risk. I don’t think these interests need to collapse into a single narrow label.
