RESEARCH TOPIC / 04

Computational Modeling and Efficiency

I work with finite-element modeling and optimization to explore efficient structural designs. This includes studying the trade-offs between structural weight, response constraints, and computational effort.

MASTER’S TERM PROJECT

Metaheuristic optimization of a 3D steel truss

Developed a vectorized direct-stiffness solver in Python and coupled it to a modified particle swarm optimizer. The design problem considered steel weight under stress, displacement, and buckling constraints across gravity, lateral, and combined load cases.

The implementation explored adaptive inertia, penalties for infeasible designs, and recovery from stagnation while handling unstable structural configurations.
